Zach V1
Glass Informatics Platform · Version 1.0

Zach V1 is a modern glass informatics platform built on top of the SciGlass database. It combines a clean interface for exploring 419,000+ glass compositions with machine-learning property prediction, viscosity model fitting, and inverse composition design — all running locally on your machine.

Designed for glass scientists, materials researchers, and process engineers who need fast, rigorous access to glass property data and computational tools.

Credits — SciGlass Database
Open Database License (ODbL) · EPAM Systems 2019

The glass composition and property data powering this platform comes from the SciGlass database, developed over decades by scientists at EPAM Systems and its predecessors. SciGlass is the world's largest curated database of inorganic glass properties, containing data on over 400,000 glass compositions from scientific literature published between 1920 and 2019.

The database is released under the Open Database License (ODbL), which permits free use, modification, and redistribution — including for commercial purposes — provided attribution is given and derivative databases are shared under the same license.

Scientific Methods
• MYEGA viscosity model — Mauro, Yue, Ellison, Gupta & Allan (2009)
• VFT equation — Vogel (1921), Fulcher (1925), Tammann (1926)
• Optical basicity — Duffy & Ingram (1971, 1975)
• NBO/T — Mysen, Richet (2005)
• Topological constraint theory — Phillips (1979), Thorpe (1983)
• Bond strength classification — Zachariasen (1932), Sun (1947)
• Avramov-Milchev model — Avramov & Milchev (1988)
• Random Forest regression — Breiman (2001)
